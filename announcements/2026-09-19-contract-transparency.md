# AQT contract and trading transparency update

**19 September 2026 — 210 CodeLabs**

The AQT contract is verified on Ethereum and uses a fixed one-billion-token supply. It has no mint function, transfer tax, blacklist, arbitrary balance adjustment, or proxy upgrade mechanism. The Safe owner can pause and unpause transfers as an emergency control.

An independent fresh-wallet test recorded a buy, approval, and successful sale through the AQT/WETH pool:

- [Buy](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x9da016206c3930569bd2cc05e73182a7510fcd1d214fc6b7fb4b1ac85e844a60)
- [Approval](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x5c77d7c60b39eb554878af09218676f3ec32c993073c8bf718d16072c9f36e83)
- [Sale](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x343057d75fe5f92b1b7ebb2c23e9ea720d51fbaedfc917f07db4b3dac8ee15c5)

The test shows that the token was sellable at that time. It does not guarantee future liquidity, price, routing, or availability. See the [risk disclosures](../RISK_DISCLOSURES.md) before interacting with the token.
