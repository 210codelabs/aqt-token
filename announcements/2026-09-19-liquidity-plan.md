# AQT liquidity and distribution plan published

**19 September 2026 — 210 CodeLabs**

The project has published a plan for gradual liquidity expansion, multisignature custody, LP administration, and a future public lock arrangement. The current AQT/WETH pool remains limited and should be treated as unlocked until a lock transaction is published.

The plan records the current Safe custody, the recovered treasury transfer, the operational distributor, the intended liquidity sequence, and the conditions for future vesting or timelock contracts.

[Read the full plan](../LIQUIDITY_AND_DISTRIBUTION_PLAN.md)

No target date or guaranteed amount is being represented as completed. Any completed liquidity, LP lock, or vesting action will be linked to its Ethereum transaction.
